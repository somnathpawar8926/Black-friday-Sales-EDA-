# 🛍️ Black Friday Sales Analysis | Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Black Friday sales data to understand customer purchasing behavior, product preferences, and sales patterns. Using Python and data visualization techniques, the project explores how customer demographics, product categories, and other factors relate to purchasing amounts.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior during Black Friday sales.
- Understand purchase patterns across different age groups and genders.
- Identify popular product categories.
- Explore spending patterns based on occupation and city category.
- Examine the relationship between customer demographics and purchase amounts.
- Generate meaningful insights to support business and marketing decisions.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **Python** – Data analysis and processing.
- **Pandas** – Data cleaning and manipulation.
- **NumPy** – Numerical computations.
- **Matplotlib** – Data visualization.
- **Seaborn** – Statistical visualization.
- **Jupyter Notebook** – Interactive data analysis.

## 🔍 Project Workflow

1. **Data Loading:** Import the Black Friday sales dataset.
2. **Data Understanding:** Examine dataset dimensions, column types, and summary statistics.
3. **Data Cleaning:** Identify and handle missing values and data quality issues.
4. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Investigate purchasing trends and customer characteristics.
5. **Data Visualization:** Create charts to identify patterns and relationships.
6. **Insights Generation:** Summarize key findings about customer spending and product preferences.

## 📊 Key Analysis Areas

- Customer age and gender distribution.
- Purchase amounts across demographic groups.
- Product category performance.
- Spending patterns by occupation.
- City category and stay-duration analysis.
- Customer purchase behavior and product preferences.

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis helps explore customer segments, product preferences, and purchasing patterns during Black Friday sales. These insights can help businesses understand customer behavior, plan targeted marketing campaigns, improve product recommendations, and make more informed sales decisions.
